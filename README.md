# Hi, I'm Anshika 👋

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at IGDTUW. I like building things that solve a clear problem and make someone's day a little easier. My projects have taken me from local services and business tools to forecasting and AI experiments.

## Projects I have built

**[BestDentistIn](https://github.com/Anshiika123/BestDentistIn)** started with a simple question: why should finding a dentist nearby feel difficult? I built a way for people to discover local clinics and contact them directly. I enjoyed thinking through both sides of the experience: what a patient needs to know and how a clinic can keep track of new enquiries.

**[AI-CRM](https://github.com/Anshiika123/AI-CRM)** is my take on making a sales team's daily work more organised. It brings leads, conversations, and deals into one place and explores how a team might decide what to follow up on next. I enjoyed designing the flow from a first enquiry to a real customer relationship. Its lead-scoring results are based on sample data, so they are a demonstration rather than a real-world performance claim.

**[Demand Forecasting and Inventory Optimization](https://github.com/Anshiika123/Demand-Forecasting-and-Inventory-optimization)** grew out of a practical question: how can a business prepare for demand without buying too much or running out? I enjoyed connecting predictions to an actual decision about stock, instead of stopping at a chart of numbers.

**[AstroMock](https://github.com/Anshiika123/AstroMock)** let me explore a personal interest in Vedic astrology through a project people can interact with. I enjoyed turning detailed calculations into a clearer experience for someone curious about their chart.

**[AI Dev Team](https://github.com/Anshiika123/my-own-ai-agents)** is an experiment in how specialised AI agents can work together on a software project. What interested me most was how to give each agent a clear job and bring their work back together.

I'm especially interested in building useful software and learning how thoughtful product decisions can make complex ideas easier to use.
